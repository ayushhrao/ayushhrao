## 🔬 Hackathon Research & Technical Exploration

For my hackathon projects, I have worked on **technical research and exploration** around AI,
computer vision, browser technologies, and lightweight AI systems.

### 🧠 AI / ML Research

- **Large Language Models (LLMs)** — fundamentals, capabilities, use cases, and their role in AI systems.
- **Datasets** — dataset structure, preparation, and their importance for model training.
- **Vision-Language Models (VLMs)** — exploring how visual and language information can work together for AI perception.

### 🌐 Browser & Web Technologies

- **DOM** — understanding how browser agents interact with traditional web interfaces.
- **WebGL** — researching browser-based graphics rendering and canvas/WebGL interfaces.
- **WebGPU** — exploring GPU acceleration for efficient browser-based AI and vision workloads.
- **WebAssembly (WASM)** — studying high-performance computation in browser environments.

### 🤖 AI Agents & On-Device AI

- Exploring how **browser agents** perceive and interact with complex web interfaces.
- Studying **visual perception** when important information is rendered through canvas/WebGL rather than the DOM.
- Exploring **lightweight and privacy-conscious on-device AI** approaches.
- Understanding the trade-offs between **DOM-based interaction and vision-based perception**.

> **Hackathon focus:** Exploring the architecture and technologies required for lightweight,
> low-latency, privacy-conscious AI systems that can operate in browser environments.
