## 🔬 Research & Hackathon Work

Alongside development, I have worked on **technical research and exploration for hackathon projects**, focusing on the intersection of **AI, computer vision, browser technologies, and AI agents**.

### 🧠 AI & Machine Learning

- **Large Language Models (LLMs)** — studying their fundamentals, capabilities, and role in AI systems.
- **Datasets** — exploring dataset structure, preparation, and their importance in training AI/ML models.
- **Vision-Language Models (VLMs)** — exploring how visual and language information can be combined for AI-based perception.

### 🌐 Browser & Web Technologies

- **DOM** — understanding browser-agent interaction with traditional web interfaces and the limitations of DOM-based interaction.
- **WebGL** — researching browser-based graphics rendering and visual interfaces.
- **WebGPU** — exploring GPU acceleration and its potential for efficient AI and computer-vision workloads in the browser.
- **WebAssembly (WASM)** — studying how high-performance computation can be executed in browser environments.

### 🤖 AI Agents & On-Device AI

- Exploring **browser agents** and how they can perceive and interact with complex web interfaces.
- Studying **visual perception for AI agents** when information is rendered through canvas or WebGL rather than the DOM.
- Exploring **lightweight and privacy-conscious on-device AI approaches** for browser environments.
- Researching the technical trade-offs between **DOM-based interaction and vision-based perception**.

> **Hackathon focus:** Understanding the technologies and architecture required to build lightweight, low-latency, and privacy-conscious AI systems for browser-based environments.
